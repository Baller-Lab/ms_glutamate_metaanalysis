### Brief Project Description:
We systematically review magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS) studies to determine whether brain glutamate differs between individuals with MS and healthy controls (HC), and if this relates to tissue-specific injury, clinical phenotype, and clinical outcomes. Of 198 studies screened, 31 (91 samples) met criteria, including 1,111 patients with MS and 743 HC. The primary outcome was glutamate concentration in MS versus HC, overall and stratified by tissue type and clinical phenotype. Secondary outcomes were associations between glutamate and anxiety, depression, and global disability.
